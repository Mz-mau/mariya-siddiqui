ABOUT ME

About me

I’m Mariya Siddiqui, a youth advocate and AI speaker based in Western Australia, and Chief Operating Officer at The Alina Lab.

My advocacy focuses on inclusion and student wellbeing. I’m a CALD Ambassador with Rotary International, and I founded Step Up & Shine, a student-led podcast on bullying prevention and peer support.

I’ve also built SecureAge, an AI-powered age verification prototype, and won the Cyber Security award at Young ICT Explorers.
